# Company overview

Fernway is a remote-first B2B SaaS company, founded in 2024, registered in Berlin. The team is spread across five countries.

## Product

**Fernway Shifts** — shift planning and labour-cost forecasting for independent café and restaurant groups with 3–20 locations. Managers build schedules; the tool checks them against local break and overtime rules and projects the wage bill before the week is published.

## Customers and markets

Germany, the Netherlands, Ireland.

~40 accounts / ~140 paying venues, as of 2026-09-22, taken from the company context pack and not yet verified. Source of truth: HubSpot (accounts), Stripe (venues).

## Positioning

Fernway's pitch: competitors are built for enterprise chains and take weeks to configure; a Fernway venue is live in under a day. Onboarding speed is the whole positioning, which is why T2L (time to live) is the metric the team watches — see [onboarding/t2l.md](../onboarding/t2l.md) and [glossary.md](../glossary.md#t2l).

**Open decision:** the "live in under a day" claim and the onboarding flow's actual typical T2L of 1–3 days don't match. Not resolved here — see [decisions.md](../decisions.md#6-t2l-claim-vs-target).
