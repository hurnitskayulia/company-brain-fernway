# Inbound lead flow

1. Lead comes in via the website form or an Intercom chat.
2. Lands in HubSpot.
3. Priya qualifies it.
4. Daniel runs the demo — see [sales/demo.md](demo.md).
5. Mara joins the call only for deals above 10 venues.

**Open decision:** how fast a lead must be qualified. Sofia's stated target is 24 hours; Priya's actual practice in busy weeks is 48 hours, and the team has quietly accepted it. Neither is the confirmed rule. See [decisions.md](../decisions.md#1-lead-qualification-target).
