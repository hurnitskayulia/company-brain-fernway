# New customer onboarding

Owner: Lukas. Typical time to live (T2L): 1–3 days — see [onboarding/t2l.md](t2l.md).

1. Contract signed in HubSpot.
2. Lukas books a 45-minute setup call.
3. Imports the venue and staff list from the customer's CSV — see [onboarding/importer.md](importer.md) for known failures.
4. Configures pay rules for the country — see [onboarding/pay-rules.md](pay-rules.md).
5. Customer publishes their first schedule.
6. 7-day check-in call.
7. Handover to support.
